Reset or delete account

Reset clears the selected account's assessments, evidence and improvement plans and keeps its sign-in, email and organisations. Delete removes the account, its email and all its data, including its External ID sign-in user. Neither can be undone. Approve by typing the account email and the action word; the approval lasts 15 minutes in this staff session and works once.
