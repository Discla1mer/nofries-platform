For consultants and IT providers

Use No Fries with your clients

If you advise small businesses on IT or security, you can run the No Fries cyber check for each client from one account, and refer clients who want hands-on help.

What you can do today

  • Run the free check for clients. Choose Consultant when you sign up. Your free plan covers your own workspace and up to 3 client organisations.
  • Keep each client separate. Every client organisation has its own assessment, results and improvement plan.
  • Share the results. Copy or print the summary for the client’s board, insurer or a customer questionnaire.

Create a free consultant account

Referring a client

When a client you refer buys a No Fries service, No Fries contracts with the client, sends the invoices and provides the support.

  • You earn 20% of what that client pays, excluding GST, for 12 months from their first payment.
  • We confirm the arrangement in writing before you refer anyone.

Paid assessments for your clients

NIST CSF 2.0 and ISO 27001 readiness self-assessments are a paid add-on. Advisers pay the same rate per client organisation as any organisation, and we invoice the adviser. See pricing.

What is not offered yet

  • Volume discounts or a reseller price list.
  • Your own branding on client results.
  • More than one sign-in per organisation.

If one of these would decide it for you, tell us. It helps us choose what to build next.

Talk to us

To set up a referral arrangement or ask about more client organisations, use the contact form. We usually reply within one business day.
