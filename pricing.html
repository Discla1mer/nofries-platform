90-day managed security

NZ$1,250 + GST for 90 days

Who makes the changes

For each improvement, the agreed scope needs to say whether No Fries, you or your IT provider makes the change. Hands-on changes by No Fries need to be separately agreed in that scope. Managed IT is priced separately.

Add-on NIST CSF 2.0 and ISO 27001 readiness self-assessments

The free dashboard and the plans above cover the NCSC-aligned assessment. The NIST CSF 2.0 and ISO 27001 readiness self-assessments are available as a paid add-on.

What is included

One plan includes both the NIST CSF 2.0 and ISO 27001 readiness self-assessments. There is no per-framework charge.

How to get access

We invoice manually, then our staff enable access. No payment card is needed.

Support for paid plans

Paid-plan support is by email during New Zealand business hours. We reply to support requests within two business days. New enquiries through Contact usually get a reply within one business day.

Support does not include incident response. It is not certification.
