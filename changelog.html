Published information and changes

Oct–Dec 2026

Fictional readiness examples

Published

Two fictional examples illustrate possible NIST CSF 2.0 and ISO 27001 readiness results using made-up information. These examples do not provide access to the paid assessments.

Clearer managed-security responsibilities

Published

The pricing page now explains that, for each improvement, the agreed scope says whether No Fries, you or your IT provider makes the change, and that hands-on changes by No Fries must be agreed separately. The clarification preserves the agreed service scope and existing terms.

Read the service details

NIST CSF 2.0 and ISO 27001 readiness self-assessments

Published

Paid NIST CSF 2.0 and ISO 27001 readiness self-assessments are now available. The ISO 27001 readiness self-assessment is not certification. For accredited certification, use an accredited certification body.

See pricing and how to get access

Paid assessment status for advisers

Published

Advisers can see paid assessment status for the client organisations they are authorised to manage. They see status only, not answers or results.

Simpler sign-up

Published

Setting up your organisation now asks for its name and a confirmation that you are authorised to set it up. We ask for your name, role and business address after your first result, before you share a board or insurer summary or request a service.
