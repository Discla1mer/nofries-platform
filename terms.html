Dashboard terms

Using the No Fries Dashboard

Last updated: 3 November 2026. No Fries Limited, Auckland, New Zealand, operates the dashboard. It helps businesses record their cyber security practices, understand gaps and discuss improvements with their IT provider.

Your organisation’s agreement and price

Creating an account or verifying your email does not accept service terms or activate a subscription. Before activation, an authorised representative reviews the dashboard agreement and access schedule identifying the organisation, included access, limits, dates and agreed price, including any complimentary access. Those accepted documents govern your organisation’s service; this page does not replace them.

We will not charge for a subscription, convert complimentary access to paid access or purchase services for you without your separate express agreement to the relevant price and scope. Managed IT and other services are optional and separately agreed.

What assessment results mean

Results reflect the answers you supply against the stated assessment framework. They are a starting point for improvements, not independent verification, National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC) endorsement, certification or a guarantee of security or compliance. Check findings with someone who knows your systems.

Only enter information you are authorised to share. Do not enter passwords, access tokens or other secrets. See our privacy information for account, assessment and support data handling.

Questions about dashboard access, the agreement or your results: hello@nofries.nz.

Separate staff evaluation workspace terms

The following terms apply to the private sales discovery and proposal evaluation workspace, not the customer dashboard. Access does not create a production service commitment, procurement arrangement, or licence for wider organisational use.

Approved access

You may use the application only through your own approved Microsoft identity and assigned role. Do not share access, attempt to reach another tenant, bypass controls, or use another person’s identity.

Evaluation scope

The workspace is an early-stage sales discovery and proposal tool. It supports consultant judgement; it does not provide legal, audit, compliance, financial, or security assurance, and it does not replace technical validation or formal scope approval.

Information and permissions

Only enter information you are authorised to use in this external application. Do not upload credentials, secrets, raw security configurations, or restricted customer information without explicit written approval. Use fictional or specifically approved records during a pilot.

Human review

Service matches are deterministic suggestions based on the approved catalogue and recorded answers. A consultant must review, accept, reject, or validate each recommendation. Nothing is sent to a customer or CRM automatically.

Service catalogue and product material

No Fries methods, question structures, matching logic, application code, templates, and reusable outputs may not be copied, republished, reverse engineered, resold, or used to create a competing product except where mandatory law permits. Tenant-provided service material remains subject to the tenant’s ownership and use restrictions.

CRM actions

CRM handoff requires an approved proposal, an explicit user confirmation, and a configured tenant-controlled endpoint. Users remain responsible for checking the account, owner, values, and next action before handoff and for correcting CRM records when necessary.

Availability and changes

The pilot is provided on an evaluation basis and may change, pause, or be withdrawn. Keep source material and final approved customer deliverables in the organisation’s authorised systems of record.

Suspension and deletion

Access may be suspended for security, misuse, lack of approval, or end of pilot. Administrators may delete pilot records in accordance with the agreed retention approach.

Contact

Questions about these terms or pilot access can be sent to hello@nofries.nz.
