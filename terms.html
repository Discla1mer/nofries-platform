Dashboard service and assessment limits

The dashboard supports your organisation's manual cyber security assessment, saving and resuming answers, and viewing results calculated from submitted answers using the identified framework and scoring version. It supports planning and informed decisions. Results depend on the information supplied; they are not an independent audit, verification of implementation, compliance opinion, certification or guarantee against a security incident.

Manual assessment and deterministic results do not require AI. Additional modules and capabilities are available only within your agreed scope and when enabled. Roadmap descriptions and previews do not add services to your agreement. Dashboard access does not include managed IT, monitoring, incident response, remediation, a human review, report exports, file uploads or external integrations unless separately agreed and enabled.

You can use the dashboard with your existing IT team and suppliers. No Fries Managed IT is a separate optional service.

Your rights under New Zealand law

New Zealand law governs these terms and your agreement.

The dashboard is supplied to organisations acquiring it in trade. Where the law allows, the agreement says that the Consumer Guarantees Act 1993 does not apply (section 43) and that sections 9, 12A and 13 of the Fair Trading Act 1986 do not apply (section 5D). Those exclusions are effective only where both parties are in trade, the agreement is in writing, and it is fair and reasonable for the parties to be bound by them. If those conditions are not met, the exclusions do not apply.

Nothing in the agreement excludes rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded, including protection under the unfair contract terms provisions of the Fair Trading Act 1986 for standard form small trade contracts, and your rights under the Privacy Act 2020. If you have questions about how a term affects you, contact us before accepting. You may ask to negotiate terms or obtain independent legal advice.

If you are acting for yourself rather than an organisation, do not create a dashboard account. Contact us instead.

Information, permissions and privacy

Only enter information you are authorised to use in this application and that is needed for the agreed assessment. Do not enter passwords, credentials, access tokens, recovery codes, raw security configurations or restricted information outside the agreed scope. Use fictional or specifically approved records where an evaluation requires them. An available text field does not authorise file uploads or access to an external system.

You remain responsible for checking assessment inputs, interpreting results and deciding what action to take. Where information concerns another person, the applicable collection authority and privacy notices must be addressed. Each party remains responsible for its own legal obligations. See the indirect collection information and Privacy notice.

Access revocation, expiry and deletion are separate. Retention, exit access and any lawful hold follow your applicable agreement and the retention information. Expiry alone does not delete saved work.
