Dashboard service and assessment limits

The dashboard supports your organisation’s manual cyber security assessment, saving and resuming answers, and viewing results calculated from submitted answers using the identified framework and scoring version. It supports planning and informed decisions. Results depend on the information supplied; they are not an independent audit, verification of implementation, compliance opinion, certification or guarantee against a security incident.

Manual assessment and deterministic results do not require AI. Additional modules and capabilities are available only within your agreed scope and when enabled. Roadmap descriptions and previews do not add services to your agreement. Dashboard access does not include managed IT, monitoring, incident response, remediation, a human review, report exports, file uploads or external integrations unless separately agreed and enabled.

You can use the dashboard with your existing IT team and suppliers. No Fries Managed IT is a separate optional service.

Your rights under New Zealand law

New Zealand law governs these terms and your agreement.

The dashboard is supplied to organisations acquiring it in trade. It is not for personal, domestic or household use. If you are acting for yourself rather than an organisation, do not create a dashboard account. Contact us instead.

The Consumer Guarantees Act 1993 sets guarantees for supplies in trade to consumers. A business customer may contract out of that Act under section 43 only where the contracting out is done properly: both parties are in trade, the agreement is in writing, and it is fair and reasonable for the parties to be bound by it. Where the law allows, the agreement says that the Consumer Guarantees Act 1993 does not apply. If those conditions are not met, the exclusion does not apply.

The Fair Trading Act 1986 prohibits misleading and deceptive conduct, unsubstantiated representations, and false or misleading representations (sections 9, 12A and 13). Where the law allows, the agreement says that those sections do not apply (section 5D). That exclusion is effective only where both parties are in trade, the agreement is in writing, and it is fair and reasonable for the parties to be bound by it. If those conditions are not met, the exclusion does not apply, and sections 9, 12A and 13 still apply. An exclusion is not permission to make a misleading claim where the Act still applies.

Nothing in the agreement excludes rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded, including protection under the unfair contract terms provisions of the Fair Trading Act 1986 for standard form small trade contracts, and your rights under the Privacy Act 2020.

The Contract and Commercial Law Act 2017 applies to these terms and your agreement, including electronic communications and contractual remedies. This page does not add a remedy, remove one, or change the liability, suspension, termination or refund provisions in your accepted agreement.
