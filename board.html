Board questions

The board asks, “Are we OK on cyber security?”

For your next board meeting, write down when your team last restored a file from backup. If nobody knows, record that as an open question.

  • Multi-factor authentication (MFA): an extra sign-in check for email and key accounts.
  • Backups: know what is covered and when a restore was last tested.
  • Patching: know who keeps work computers and software up to date.
  • Recovery: know who takes charge and what to do if systems go down.
