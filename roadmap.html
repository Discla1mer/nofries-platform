What’s next.

What you can use today, what is being built and what is planned.

NZ financial-year quarters are planning windows, not delivery commitments. Planned scope, order and availability may change; a quarter does not promise delivery or subscription inclusion.

What you can use depends on the access your organisation has agreed.

90-day managed security includes a written summary you can give your board or insurer now. That summary is part of the paid service. We work through three improvements with you. For each improvement, the agreed scope needs to say whether No Fries, you or your IT provider makes the change. Hands-on changes by No Fries need to be separately agreed in that scope. Managed IT is the separate day-to-day service. See service details.
