What’s next.

What you can use today, what is being built and what is planned.

Last updated 25 November 2026.

Planned work has no confirmed dates. Scope, order and availability may change, and planned work does not promise subscription inclusion.

What you can use depends on the access your organisation has agreed.

Managed IT and 90-day managed security are separate services. See 90-day service details.

  1. Done

    Oct–Dec 2026

    Free NCSC dashboard

    Create a Company account, save and resume the 14-question NCSC assessment, and compare submitted results over time. Free dashboard; it doesn’t expire. Existing agreed offers and their terms remain unchanged.

    Results with your top three gaps

    See gaps in your saved answers, with reasons and next steps. Multi-factor authentication, patching, backups and payment-change checks come first. Copy or print a summary to share.

    See a fictional result

    Improvement actions

    Give assessment gaps an owner, due date and status. Carry those into the next assessment’s plan. A completed task does not prove that a control works.

    Try the demo

    Explore a fictional business before creating an account. Entries stay in your browser on this device until you choose Reset demo; they are not sent to No Fries.

    Try the demo

    Ask about a service

    Open the contact form from a service in the dashboard, with that service already named.

    Welcome and result emails

    Receive a welcome email when you create an account and a link to your result when you submit an assessment.

    Fictional readiness examples

    Two fictional readiness examples show what results for the paid assessments could look like. They use made-up information.

    Paid readiness self-assessments

    NIST CSF 2.0 and ISO 27001 readiness self-assessments are available as a paid add-on. The ISO 27001 readiness self-assessment is not certification. For accredited certification, use an accredited certification body.

    See pricing and how to get access

    Paid assessment status for advisers

    Advisers can see paid assessment status for the client organisations they are authorised to manage. They see status only, not answers or results.

    Simpler sign-up

    Set up your organisation with its name and a confirmation that you are authorised to set it up. We ask for your name, role and business address after your first result, before you share a board or insurer summary or request a service.

    Consultant dashboard after sign-in

    Fixed an issue that could stop consultants opening their dashboard after signing in, while it was checking paid assessment status.

    A reference for dashboard support

    Some temporary problems loading your dashboard now show a support reference. If you see one, give it to No Fries when you ask for help.

  2. In progress

    Returning to an unfinished sign-up

    In progress

    Planned guidance that brings an interrupted sign-up back to the same account. These changes are still being checked.

    Updated NCSC questions

    In progress

    A planned 16-question assessment with weighted scoring. Existing submitted results keep their original scoring.

    Ask No Fries to contact you

    In progress

    A planned request button on results and Services that explains what staff will see before you choose it. Nothing is shared through this request unless you ask. This is separate from the existing link to the contact form.

  3. Next

    Review of not applicable answers

    Planned

    A planned way to review and confirm answers marked not applicable. For now, these answers count as not yet met and stay in your result; no review changes that.

    Update submitted answers

    Planned

    Planned ways to update submitted answers and refresh the result without starting again.

    Evidence and board reporting

    Planned

    Planned evidence references, owners, recurring reviews and downloadable assessment reports. Customer uploads and a report you can download yourself are not available yet. A reference or completed review does not independently verify a control.

    Assess against your own requirements

    Planned

    Planned question review alongside the source requirements, with customer-specific results kept separate from NCSC results. Text import is being developed; PDF, DOCX and scanned-document imports are later options.

    Email reminders

    Planned

    Planned unfinished-assessment nudges, reassessment reminders and monthly status emails. Settings and unsubscribe links exist; reminders still need testing and activation.

  4. Later

    Separate assessment types

    Planned

    Planned CIS, Essential Eight and distinct ASD Essentials assessments, plus a view of how answers map to SMB1001. Each assessment would keep its own version, scope and results; a mapping is not certification.

    Connected evidence and continuous checks

    Planned

    Planned connections to supported systems, with scope, freshness and missing data visible. Potential sources include Microsoft 365, Datto RMM and managed detection and response. These are not active integrations or included services.

    Controls, evidence and review workflows

    Planned

    Planned reusable controls, traceable evidence, risk exceptions, policies and approvals. Longer-term work covers assets, access, suppliers, people, recovery and technical reviews. Claims, technical checks and independent assurance would remain distinct.

    Questionnaires and privacy responsibilities

    Planned

    Planned reuse of reviewed answers for customer questionnaires and preparation for independent SOC 2 assessment. Track data handling, retention and deletion responsibilities. Legal decisions stay separate from technical evidence; No Fries does not issue an independent assurance opinion.

    Client and service overview

    Planned

    Planned status views for agreed No Fries services across the client organisations a consultant is authorised to manage.

    AI governance and IT optimisation

    Planned

    Planned ways to understand AI use and identify opportunities to improve IT.

Use results to plan improvements. They are not NCSC endorsement, independent assurance, certification or a guarantee of compliance. Planned capabilities are not a promise of delivery or subscription inclusion.
