Live Available now

Resume an assessment

Sign in and reopen saved assessments for existing Company accounts with enabled access. This does not confirm access for every account.

NCSC cyber assessments

Answer questions, save progress and compare your submitted answers with NCSC guidelines. Results reflect your answers; they are not independently verified compliance.

In development Work under way

Reliable signup and support access

We are testing fresh signup, access to the correct organisation, saving an assessment, signing out and returning to saved progress and results.

A separate signup viewer for authorised No Fries staff is also being prepared. It is not a complete account-management or administration portal.

Update answers and compare results

Edit a submitted assessment and refresh your scores. Compare the new results with earlier assessments.

Assess against your own requirements

Import client requirements from text, review each question alongside its source and return to saved work. Keep customer-specific results separate from NCSC assessments.

Work to make this available inside the dashboard is under way. PDF, DOCX and scanned-document imports are planned.

Improvement actions

Turn saved assessment gaps into actions with accountable owners, due dates, status and change history. Track remediation and retesting without treating a completed task as proof that a control works.

The dashboard workflow is being built and tested; it is not available to customers yet.

Evidence and board reporting

Work is under way on retaining evidence records and preparing assessment snapshots for reports. Customer uploads, evidence validation and downloadable reports are not available yet.

Evidence references and recurring reviews

Record evidence references, accountable owners and review due dates, then return to recurring reviews. A reference or completed review does not establish that a control has been independently verified.

These workflows are being built and tested; they are not released.

Separate assessment types

Work is under way on separate, versioned assessment types, including NIST CSF and NZ NCSC. CIS, ISO 27001 readiness, Essential Eight and a distinct ASD Essentials assessment are planned. Each assessment type will retain its own version, scope and results.

This expanded assessment work is not released. The existing NCSC assessment remains available as described above.

Ready for release Awaiting publication

No additional capability is listed here yet. Work moves here only after its release checks are complete.

Planned A wider compliance platform

A versioned control library

Bring NCSC, CIS, ISO and SOC2 control references into a versioned library. Map client requirements and their sources to relevant controls, with the mapping rationale and reviewer recorded.

Record which controls apply, who is accountable, who reviews them and how much confidence the supporting evidence provides.

Evidence you can trace and review

Keep uploaded and collected evidence over time, with its source, origin, collection date, freshness and review history. Distinguish submitted claims, technical checks, reviewer decisions and independent assurance.

Connected evidence and continuous checks

Connect supported systems and repeat control checks showing which systems, assets and people are covered and when evidence was collected. Missing, stale, disconnected or incomplete data must remain a gap, not a pass.

Potential connectors include Datto RMM and patching evidence, and managed detection and response (MDR) evidence. These are planned options, not active integrations or included subscription services.

Risk and exceptions

Record risks, treatment decisions and approvals. Give exceptions an owner, reason, review date and expiry while retaining the original finding.

Policies and approvals

Manage policy versions, accountable owners, approvals, records of staff reading each policy and scheduled reviews.

Assets, identities and devices

Link asset and data inventories to owners and lifecycle records. Review access, joiners and leavers, multifactor authentication, device encryption, supported software, patching and endpoint protection evidence.

Show coverage and exceptions across devices, applications, networks, email and data protection, including physical locations and media where relevant.

Supplier and personnel security

Keep supplier assurance, review and renewal evidence alongside staff training and appropriate screening-completion records. Track gaps across the suppliers and people in scope.

Recovery and incident readiness

Review backup and restore evidence, recovery exercises and measured recovery objectives. Track incident plans, response exercises, responsibilities and reviewed notification decisions.

Technical assurance

Track vulnerability testing, secure development, software-component inventories, logging and security reviews. Keep penetration-test reports, remediation and independent retesting linked to their scope and findings.

Customer questionnaires and audit-ready reporting

Reuse reviewed answers and current evidence in customer security questionnaires. Prepare audit-ready exports and board reports that show scope, progress, gaps and exceptions without hiding incomplete checks.

Independent auditor collaboration and SOC2 readiness

Support auditor requests, collaboration with access limited to the agreed scope, and evidence tracking over review periods for SOC2 Type I and Type II readiness. Keep point-in-time evidence separate from evidence of operation over a period.

SOC2 attestation is issued by an independent qualified auditor. No Fries does not self-certify or issue the auditor’s opinion.

Privacy and data responsibilities

Track data handling, subprocessor approvals, retention and deletion obligations, and reviewed privacy requests. Keep legal decisions and permissions separate from technical evidence.

A better trial experience

Trial policy improvements are planned. Existing agreed three-month pilot offers and their terms remain unchanged.
