Invoice and bank-detail scams

That invoice looks right. Are the bank details?

If bank details change, call your supplier on a number you already know to verify the change before paying. Do not use the number on the invoice. Record who confirmed the details and when. If you cannot confirm the change, do not pay yet.

  • Second check: have another person review unusual or large payments.
  • Multi-factor authentication (MFA): add an extra sign-in check to email and finance accounts.
  • Staff awareness: make it easy to pause and query an unexpected request.
