Account access

Help signing in

If your email and password are not accepted, check that you are using the email you used to create your No Fries account. The message does not tell you whether an account exists.

Forgot your password?

No Fries uses Microsoft to handle sign-in. Continue to sign in, enter your email, then use the password-reset or forgotten-password option on the Microsoft page if it is offered. Follow the instructions sent to your email.

If you use a work or school account and cannot reset it, ask your organisation’s Microsoft account administrator for help.

Continue to sign in

Still stuck?

If no reset option is offered, or you cannot access your email, contact hello@nofries.nz. Tell us which step failed and any support reference shown. Never send your password or a sign-in code.

We cannot reset a Microsoft password for you or confirm account ownership through this page.
