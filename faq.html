Questions and answers

Common questions

Last reviewed: 28 November 2026.

What is the cyber check?

16 questions about how your business works today, adapted from New Zealand NCSC guidance. You get results in plain English, with your top gaps first.

How long does it take?

About 15 minutes. You can save and come back.

What does it cost?

The check is free. If you want help fixing the gaps, 90-day managed security is NZ$1,250 + GST. See pricing.

Do I need to be technical?

No. The questions are written for owners and office managers. If you are not sure of an answer, choose “I don’t know” and we will show you what to check.

Does it scan my computers or network?

No. It is a questionnaire. Nothing is installed and nothing connects to your systems.

Is this an audit or a certification?

No. It is a self-assessment based on your answers. It helps you decide what to fix first.

Is No Fries part of the NCSC?

No. The questions are adapted from NCSC guidance, but No Fries is a private company and is not endorsed by the NCSC.

What happens to my answers?

They are stored in New Zealand and used to work out your results. We do not sell them or use them for marketing without asking. See our security page and privacy notice.

Can I show the results to my board, insurer or a customer?

Yes. Your results include a summary you can copy or print, and answers set out for insurer and customer questionnaires.

Who fixes the gaps?

No Fries can fix them with you. You can also take your results to whoever looks after your IT.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. Ask us to export or delete your data: see Access, correction and complaints.

I advise several businesses. Can I use it for my clients?

Yes. Choose the consultant option when you sign up to manage client organisations from one account.

How do I talk to someone?

Use the contact form. We usually reply within one business day.
