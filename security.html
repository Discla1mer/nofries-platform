Security

How No Fries protects your information

Last reviewed: 28 November 2026. This page is a plain summary. The privacy notice has the full detail and takes priority if the two differ.

Where your data is stored

  • The dashboard and its assessment database are hosted in Microsoft Azure New Zealand North.
  • Sign-in sessions, signup invitations and agreement records are in private Azure Storage, also in New Zealand North.
  • Microsoft Entra External ID handles sign-in and keeps your sign-in identity, including your email address, in Australia.

Encryption

Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS and encrypted at rest by the Azure service that holds it.

Signing in

  • You sign in through Microsoft Entra External ID. No Fries never sees or stores a sign-in password.
  • You stay signed in for up to 4 hours. Sign out when you leave a shared computer.
  • Each request is limited to an approved organisation and role. Your browser cannot choose another organisation’s records; the server selects them.

Who can see your information

  • People your organisation authorises, within what their role allows.
  • No Fries staff who need it to run the service.
  • Our service providers: Microsoft for hosting and sign-in, and Resend for email. If the website assistant’s live model is switched on, messages you send it are processed by OpenAI.

We do not sell your information, use it for marketing without asking, or use it to train AI. The assessment does not use AI.

What the check does not do

The cyber check is a questionnaire. It does not scan your network, install software or connect to your systems. Do not enter passwords, API keys or other secrets anywhere in No Fries.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your information while your account is active and for the periods in your agreement. You can ask us to export or delete it: see Access, correction and complaints.

If something goes wrong

If a privacy breach is likely to cause serious harm, we notify the people affected and the Privacy Commissioner as soon as practicable, as the Privacy Act 2020 requires.

Report a security problem

Email hello@nofries.nz with the subject “No Fries security report”. Our contact details for researchers are also in security.txt. Please do not include passwords or customer data in your report.

What we do not claim

No Fries does not hold a security certification such as ISO 27001 or SOC 2, and no system can promise it will never be accessed without authority.
