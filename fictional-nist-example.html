Fictional example

NIST CSF 2.0 results

In this fictional example, a volunteer costume library looks at how it protects its bookings. Its next step is to find out how long it takes to get them back.

How the library looks after bookings

Govern. The committee named Mere, the bookings lead, as the owner of the booking system.

Identify. The team listed its booking records, volunteer contacts and payment provider.

Protect. Booking administrators use two-step sign-in.

Detect. The duty coordinator checks booking alerts each morning.

Respond. Volunteers have the provider’s contact details and a checklist to follow if they get locked out.

Recover. The team has a backup but has never timed a restore.

Time a test restore

Before the next committee meeting, Mere will restore a test copy of the bookings. She will record how long it takes and keep the dated result for the committee.
