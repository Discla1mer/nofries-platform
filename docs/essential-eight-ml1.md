# Essential Eight: Maturity Level One self-check

This profile is a small-business conversation starter for **Appendix A of the November 2023 Essential Eight maturity model**. It is not the newer Essentials series, an ASD assessment, or a finding that a business has achieved a maturity level.

The 29 questions group related checks under the eight named strategies. Read “Yes” as covering the whole stated scope, “Partly” as covering some systems or steps, “No” as absent, and “Unknown” as needing confirmation. Unknown is not a negative answer or an exclusion. Use the original model for full requirements, exceptions and assessment decisions. There is no maturity award from averaging answers.
