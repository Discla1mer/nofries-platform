# Held proposal (a) — ISO issued artifact storage

0031 depends on #502's 0030. Approval for 0028–0030 does not approve 0031. The sibling paid-plan and scope-binding proposals reserve 0032 and 0033. [Issuance design #510](https://github.com/Discla1mer/nofries-platform/pull/510).

An artifact freezes the exact UTF-8 format-1 string from ISO-5's `projectIsoOutput`. Each board summary and Statement of Applicability has a separate report ID. The database checks its kind, format and SHA-256 and pins the parent revision using the complete tenant, organisation, profile, version, assessment and revision key. An insert must match the submitted stamp, response-set digest, stored record digest and both catalogue pins for the reviewed ISO publication. Keeping the unmodified string preserves byte ordering and whitespace. The database does not rerun the assessment evaluator or renderer.

Each artifact records its maker. Approval and issuance are separate, append-only decisions against its exact report ID and output digest. A predecessor FK requires the approval to remain present while an issue exists. The two decisions enforce the order of timestamps and the monthly Proof rules for distinct canonical subjects and, when available, distinct normalized email hashes. Store SHA-256 of the verified normalized email without the raw address. Only an issue decision on the exact artifact is evidence of issuance. A new source or different bytes require a new report ID and new decisions.

**Access and erasure.** Both tables force row-level security. No application reader, writer or issuer receives access. The existing wipe role can read only the tenant ID and delete only rows for its selected wipe tenant. Staff reset/delete removes decisions, then artifacts, then their source revisions. Keep that order with the flag off and when composing the sibling proposals. Reset retains signup receipts and sign-in scope. Neither flag changes nor a privilege rollback recover erased data.

**Rollout and rollback.** Apply 0031 only after a separate approval, after 0030 and before the updated wipe code. Keep delivery disabled. To retire it, stop future writers and readers, keep erasure working while rows remain, and verify an approved retention/disposition and recovery plan. Coordinate a staff wipe maintenance window for changing the wipe list and retiring the schema. Remove decisions before artifacts, then the two trigger functions. Do not drop source revisions or alter monthly Proof. The migration ledger and backups require the operator's standard rollback process.

**Independent review before any writer or export.** Verify authoritative issuer-to-dashboard scope mapping and immutable actor identity (including email alias checks), authentic sealed-record validation by #507, and exact reconstruction of #512's reviewed bytes. Recheck staff erasure, foreign-key order, privileges and tenant isolation on the final composed heads. An eventual serving route must separately enforce exact paid entitlement, the existing immutable issued-report read permission, representative-details sharing gates, session generation/disclosure and existing quota reservation/replay. This storage proposal adds no serving route, issuer role, quota change or Copy/Print adapter.
