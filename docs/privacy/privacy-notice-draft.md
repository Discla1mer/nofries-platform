## What we collect and why

When you use an enabled feature, we collect the information needed for that feature:

- Account and sign-in information, such as your email, identity-provider identifiers, verification status and organisation membership, to identify you and control access.
- Organisation and representative details, authority declarations, invitations and accepted terms/schedules, to set up and administer the service and record agreements.
- Assessment answers, explanations, evidence references, results and improvement actions, to prepare and maintain your organisation's assessment. Free text may identify you or other people. Please avoid passwords, secrets and unnecessary personal details.
- Enquiries, support requests, assistant conversations and associated summaries or follow-up records, when you use those services, to respond and maintain the service relationship.
- Session, request and audit information to operate the service, investigate problems, prevent misuse and record significant actions. Some identifiers remain linkable to your account.

## Access, correction and complaints

You may ask whether we hold personal information about you, request access, or request correction using the privacy contact above. We will take proportionate steps to verify your identity or representative's authority. Please do not email passwords or unsolicited identity-document scans.

We will decide access requests as soon as reasonably practicable and within the applicable statutory period, generally 20 working days, subject to lawful extensions. Information granted must be supplied without undue delay. We may need to protect other people's information or apply a lawful withholding ground; we will explain the decision and available review rights. **[LEGAL: approve the request/extension/charge and refusal wording against the operating procedure.]**

If we do not make a correction you request, you can ask us to attach your statement of correction. Appropriate recipient notification and linkage of corrections to historical records will be considered. Account closure or an organisation administrator's removal of access is different from erasure. The Privacy Act does not give a general right to deletion; we will assess removal requests and retention obligations rather than promise immediate erasure of every copy.

If you are unhappy with our response, contact our privacy officer; you can also contact the [Office of the Privacy Commissioner](https://www.privacy.org.nz/). **[JOEL: confirm escalation owner and accessible alternative request channel.]**
