# Customer #1: paid-access decision

**Recommendation for Joel; not an implemented billing feature.** Reviewed 5 November 2026 NZDT. No billing account, key, payment link, invoice, charge or entitlement was created. Commercial terms and privacy wording need Joel's approval and the legal review in [#74](https://github.com/Discla1mer/nofries-platform/pull/74).

## Simplest path

Use an agreed, fixed first term, an invoice from Joel's approved existing invoicing process, and NZ bank transfer to the business's verified account. Joel verifies receipt against the invoice and customer organisation before a restricted operator activates the agreed entitlement. Review renewal before expiry; do not offer automatic renewal yet. This avoids a payment-provider integration for one customer, **not** the need to prove safe paid-term activation. If no supported activation/renewal operation is qualified, do not promise ongoing paid dashboard access or improvise a database edit.

| Option | Cost/effort and suitability |
| --- | --- |
| Invoice + bank transfer | Manual reconciliation and renewal; bank/accounting fees depend on Joel's existing arrangements. Best initial fit if the customer accepts it. No automatic entitlement from an invoice being sent or marked due. |
| Stripe-hosted one-off Payment Link or customer-specific invoice | Convenient card collection with limited payment UI engineering, but still needs provider onboarding and an app entitlement handoff. A reusable link/payment email is not proof of the payer's tenant. Payment Links are included in standard Payments pricing; separate invoicing features can add fees. |
| Stripe Billing subscription + hosted Checkout/portal | Better recurring collection and customer billing management, with more lifecycle, webhook, reconciliation and support work. NZ standard card rates currently show **2.65% + NZ$0.30 domestic**, **3.5% + NZ$0.30 international**, plus **2% if currency conversion is required**; pay-as-you-go Billing adds **0.7% of Billing volume**. Recheck actual contracted/product fees before adoption. |

Sources checked 5 November: [NZ Payments pricing](https://stripe.com/nz/pricing), [dated NZ fee change](https://support.stripe.com/questions/lower-pricing-for-new-zealand-card-transactions-effective-starting-december-1-2025), [Payment Links](https://stripe.com/nz/payments/payment-links), [NZ Billing pricing](https://stripe.com/nz/billing/pricing), [hosted invoice versus link](https://docs.stripe.com/payment-links). Use the current NZ Payments page/change notice for card rates; the Billing page's ancillary card-rate example still shows older rates. Joel/accountant must confirm GST status and required [taxable supply information](https://www.ird.govt.nz/gst/tax-invoices-for-gst); this note does not set a price or tax treatment.

## Minimum app gate and integration points

Public main reviewed: `00bf0f0f85990bc95f9bf060b612e934ea60894b`. Follow current code, not older “source-only” headers: [entitlementDomain](../../api/shared/appLifecycle/entitlementDomain.js) is used by the [PostgreSQL repository](../../api/shared/appLifecycle/postgresDashboardRepository.js) and [starter signup](../../lib/starterSignupProduction.js). Conversely, public checkout/confirmation/webhook handlers explicitly return `billing_disabled` in [apiRuntime](../../api/shared/apiRuntime.js).

1. **Keep identity/signup separate from payment.** Verification, organisation creation and accepting exact terms/schedules establish identity/authority and agreement; they do not prove payment. Preserve the existing starter/trial policy. Attach a paid order to verified immutable subject, tenant and organisation IDs, never an email-only match or a client-supplied “paid” flag.
2. **Add a small, server-owned payment-to-entitlement bridge in a separate PR.** Record opaque order/invoice reference, agreed product/modules/profile versions and limits, NZD amount, verified payment/approved-credit decision, finite paid term, reviewer and idempotent operation ID. Keep invoice contents in the approved finance system. Proposed operational states are pending, active, grace (only if explicitly approved), expired and revoked; map them to the existing grant statuses/finite validity rather than silently changing its schema. Sending an invoice grants nothing.
3. **Reuse authoritative grant enforcement.** The repository's `entitlement.update` validates revision/history and starts usage for a new product grant. Renewal needs a new non-overlapping grant/term; do not edit historical grant terms or accepted-terms digests. Preserve membership, admission, feature flags and quotas independently. Check these server-side for paid operations; hiding a button is insufficient. Existing starter account, authority, admission and session validity must also be reconciled through an approved transition: adding a grant alone does not extend those other expiries.
4. **Admin hook:** [#47](https://github.com/Discla1mer/nofries-platform/pull/47), reviewed at `685636aea724491bb4eed2d1c974e16a709f0948`, can inspect scoped memberships/entitlements and guarded account actions. It has **no “mark paid”, invoice collection or paid-term editor**. A future preview/confirm operation should bind payment evidence, exact scope, expected revisions and operator authority, write a minimal audit receipt and read back the effective result. Editing a label or restoring membership is not payment activation. Empty-signup reset is unrelated and remains disabled.
5. **Later Stripe automation:** verify signed events, deduplicate them, tolerate retries/out-of-order delivery and reconcile authoritative provider state. Match the verified order to the exact tenant; do not grant access from a browser success redirect, `checkout.session.completed` alone for an unpaid/delayed method, or subscription `active` alone. Explicitly handle paid renewals, failed payment, grace expiry, cancellation, refunds and disputes under Joel's policy. No automatic data deletion on non-payment. See [subscription events](https://docs.stripe.com/billing/subscriptions/webhooks) and [webhook handling](https://docs.stripe.com/webhooks).

Before enabling the bridge, prove wrong-tenant denial, duplicate payment, expired/overlapping term, stale approval, concurrent usage, failed payment/renewal, refund/dispute handling, and return/sign-in after starter expiry with synthetic tests. Do not change permissions, flags or production records as part of this decision note.

## Data/privacy and Joel's decisions

Link this inventory delta to [#74's privacy/export/deletion review](https://github.com/Discla1mer/nofries-platform/blob/74c1e70d51cdb04ef67e2b49ed0851c09f43f284/docs/privacy/data-handling-readiness.md): billing contact/address if necessary, invoice/order IDs, amount/currency/tax, payment status/time and processor customer/subscription references are additional personal/commercial data. Keep card details with a hosted processor; do not put bank statements, card numbers, payment payloads or assessment content in app logs, Stripe metadata or public GitHub. Finance staff access, provider/offshore processing, dispute evidence, financial retention and residual copies need approved schedules. Account closure does not automatically erase financial records, and a failed payment must not block statutory privacy requests.

**Joel decides:** (a) invoice/bank transfer or Stripe; (b) exact paid scope, price/GST, term and payment-before-activation versus approved credit; (c) renewal reminders, grace, cancellation/refund policy and what read/export access remains after expiry; (d) who verifies payment and who approves the grant, with cover when absent; (e) finance system/provider, recipient access and retention; (f) whether the qualified paid-term bridge is ready before selling continuing dashboard access. Recommended starting choice: one clearly scoped invoiced term, manual verified activation, human renewal review, no auto-renewal promise.
