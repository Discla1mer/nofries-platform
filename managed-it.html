Plans & pricing

Three tiers. Pick your cover.

Every tier is priced per user each month, excluding GST, and includes one main work computer per user.

  • Essentials

    Everyday support

    NZ$59 + GST per user / month

    • No Fries dashboard (users, devices, licences, security)
    • AI-assisted email helpdesk, with every change approved by Joel, our founder
    • Microsoft 365 admin (new starters, leavers, passwords, licences)
    • A monthly plain-English summary and to-do list

    Microsoft 365 backup can be added as an option.

  • Recommended

    Managed

    Devices and Microsoft 365 backup

    NZ$99 + GST per user / month

    Everything in Essentials, plus:

    • Device updates and patching
    • Microsoft 365 backup set up and checked
    • Dealing with your other IT vendors
    • A quarterly check-in with Joel, our founder

  • Secure

    Security hardening and alert review

    NZ$139 + GST per user / month

    Everything in Managed, plus:

    • Security hardening (MFA, sign-in and device policies)
    • Security alerts reviewed and acted on
    • Staff phishing and scam awareness tips

Set-up from NZ$750 + GST. Extra computers NZ$20 + GST each per month, extra locations NZ$125 + GST each per month.

The standard service

Support you can understand.

The agreed scope sets out your people, computers, tools and service coverage.

  1. 01

    Day-to-day support during agreed business hours

    Help with the everyday issues that get in the way of work. Business hours are NZ business hours, Monday to Friday.

  2. 02

    No Fries dashboard (users, devices, licences, security)

    See your people, devices, licences and security position in one place.

  3. 03

    AI-assisted email helpdesk, with every change approved by Joel, our founder

    Send everyday questions by email. Nothing routine is changed until Joel, our founder, has approved it.

  4. 04

    Microsoft 365 admin (new starters, leavers, passwords, licences)

    New starters, leavers, passwords and licences kept in order.

  5. 05

    A monthly plain-English summary and to-do list

    A clear monthly summary of what happened and what to do next.
