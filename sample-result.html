Your top three gaps

Chosen by a fixed rule from your saved answers. Controls not in place come first, then partly in place, then in NCSC framework order.

  1. Multi-factor Authentication

    Answer: No

    Why it matters: Reduce account takeover risk by requiring stronger authentication where stolen credentials would cause material harm.

    What to do next: Require an extra sign-in check, such as an authenticator app or security key, on important accounts. This is multi-factor authentication (MFA).

  2. Data Recovery

    Answer: No

    Why it matters: Make recovery dependable by linking backups, supporting information, roles and tests to business recovery needs.

    What to do next: Keep protected backup copies of important systems and information, and practise getting them back within the time your business needs.

  3. Response Planning

    Answer: No

    Why it matters: Reduce confusion and delay by preparing a flexible first response and practising it before a serious event.

    What to do next: Write down who does what if a cyber problem happens, keep useful phone numbers available and practise the plan.
