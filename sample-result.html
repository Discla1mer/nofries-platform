How the gaps are ranked

Multi-factor authentication, patching, backups and checking payment changes come first, because they help protect against common attacks on small businesses, such as stolen accounts, ransomware and invoice fraud. Within each priority group, we list the checks you don’t have in place before the ones you’ve partly put in place. This is a fixed order, not a risk assessment of your business.

  1. Multi-factor Authentication

    Answer: No

    Why it matters: Reduce account takeover risk by requiring stronger authentication where stolen credentials would cause material harm.

    What to do next: Require an extra sign-in check, such as an authenticator app or security key, on important accounts. This is multi-factor authentication (MFA).

  2. Data Recovery

    Answer: No

    Why it matters: Make recovery dependable by linking backups, supporting information, roles and tests to business recovery needs.

    What to do next: Keep protected backup copies of important systems and information, and practise getting them back within the time your business needs.

  3. Least Privilege

    Answer: Partial

    Why it matters: Limit accidental or malicious change and reduce how far an attacker can move after compromising an account.

    What to do next: Give people only the access they need for their job, and update or remove it when they start, change roles or leave.

Example questionnaire answers

These four suggested answers come from the same fictional Harbourview Logistics assessment. Check the exact coverage before using an answer on an insurer or customer form.

  1. Is multi-factor authentication (MFA) required for business email and remote access?

    Suggested answer: No

    Harbourview answered No to the multi-factor authentication question.

    To answer Yes: Require MFA for business email and every remote-access route, and check for exceptions.

  2. Do you keep an offline or immutable backup copy (one that cannot be changed or deleted)?

    Suggested answer: Not answered

    This assessment does not ask for this detail.

    To answer Yes: Keep a backup copy offline or locked against changes and deletion, and confirm how it is protected.

  3. Is access limited to job needs, with separate admin accounts and checks when people join, change roles or leave?

    Suggested answer: Partly

    Harbourview answered Partial to the least privilege question.

    To answer Yes: Limit access to job needs, separate admin accounts, and regularly review access and staff changes.

  4. Are payment and bank-detail changes checked through a trusted route separate from the original request?

    Suggested answer: Yes

    In this fictional example, Harbourview answered Yes to the sensitive-request verification question.

    To answer Yes: Check payment and bank-detail changes using a known phone number or another trusted, separate route.
