About No Fries

No Fries Limited is a New Zealand company based in Auckland. We help small and medium businesses see where they stand on cyber security, and then fix the gaps with them.

What we do

  • A free cyber check. 16 questions adapted from New Zealand NCSC guidance, with results in plain English. See a sample result.
  • 90-day managed security. We work through the gaps in your result with you. See pricing.
  • Managed IT. Ongoing support for your computers, accounts and backups. See Managed IT.

Who we work with

New Zealand businesses that do not have their own security team: owners, office managers, and the consultants and IT providers who advise them.

What the check is and is not

The check is a self-assessment. It records what you tell us and compares it with NCSC guidance. It is not an audit or a certification, and it does not scan or connect to your systems. No Fries is not part of, or endorsed by, the NCSC.
